The chemistry

Elements combine in fixed ratios set by valence. Writing NH₂ from nitrogen and hydrogen ignores the need to balance atoms and the actual stable molecule NH₃. This lab uses real stoichiometry and common binary products.

Many element pairs do not react under mild conditions. That is chemistry too — noble gases, inert metals and unstable combinations are reported honestly rather than inventing a formula.
