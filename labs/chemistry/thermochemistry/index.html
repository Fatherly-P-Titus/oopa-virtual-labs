The chemistry

Enthalpy is a state function: the net ΔH depends only on initial and final states. Hess’s law lets you add thermochemical equations (scaled or reversed) to reach a target reaction. Formation enthalpies are a convenient standard path from elements.

Bond enthalpies give a quick gas-phase estimate but ignore phase changes and the fact that real bond strengths vary with molecular environment.
