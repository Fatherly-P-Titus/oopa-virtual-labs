The chemistry

Ka measures acid strength. A buffer pairs a weak acid with its conjugate base so added H⁺ or OH⁻ is absorbed with only a small pH change. Henderson–Hasselbalch links pH to the ratio [A⁻]/[HA].

When you add more strong acid or base than the buffer can absorb, the ratio collapses and pH swings — that is lost capacity. Titration curves in the Titration lab show the same idea dynamically.
