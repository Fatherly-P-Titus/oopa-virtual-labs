The chemistry

The rate law says how concentration affects speed. The order is determined experimentally; this lab lets you see the integrated consequence of each choice. Half-life is a convenient clock — constant for first-order, concentration-dependent otherwise.

Arrhenius links temperature to the rate constant through the activation energy barrier. Real mechanisms can be multi-step; here the focus is the single elementary-style rate law.
