Life of a cell

Feeding
A cell eats any food it touches. Each particle gives it energy. Sensing range lets it steer toward food nearby.
Movement
Cells swim in runs with random turns. Faster swimming finds food sooner but uses more energy.
Growth
A cell grows as its energy rises and shrinks when it runs low. Brown cells are hungry and green cells are well fed.
Reproduction
At the divide energy, a cell splits into two. Each new cell gets half the energy and starts at age zero.
Death
A cell starves when its energy reaches zero. It dies of old age if it reaches its lifespan without dividing.

Try this

  1. Follow one cell. Click a cell and watch it search, feed with a gold flash, grow, and split in two.
  2. Switch off sensing. Press No sensing. Cells only find food by bumping into it, so the population grows more slowly.
  3. Starve the dish. Press Scarce food. Cells turn brown, and red rings mark the cells that starve.
  4. See that speed has a cost. Raise swimming speed. Cells find food sooner, but each one burns energy faster.
  5. Shorten lifespan. Cells that fail to divide in time die of old age, shown by grey rings.

The model is simplified: cells pass through each other, food appears at random, and there is no mutation or waste.
