The biology

Producers (plants) turn sunlight and soil nutrients into living matter. Consumers (herbivores and carnivores) eat other living things. Decomposers break dead matter back into nutrients.

Matter goes round in a cycle: the same atoms are used again and again. Energy does not. It enters as sunlight and leaves as heat, and each step up the food chain passes on only a part of it.

The model is simplified: three groups, a single soil nutrient, no seasons and no other species.

Try this

  1. Follow the cycle. With Balanced, trace the arrows from nutrients to plants, animals, dead matter and back. Total matter stays the same.
  2. Remove the top predator. Press Hunt carnivores. Herbivores usually rise first, and then the plants fall.
  3. Slow the recyclers. Press Slow decomposers. Dead matter piles up and nutrients run low.
  4. Burn it down. Press Wildfire. Plants drop, and the animals follow after a delay.
  5. Drain the soil. Press Leaky soil. Nutrients wash away and the whole web has less to live on.
