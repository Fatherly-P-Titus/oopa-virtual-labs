Try this

  1. Watch it even out. In Diffusion, put 100 particles on the left and none on the right. The graph’s two lines meet, but the crossings per second never stop.
  2. Block it. Switch the particle to starch, or close the membrane. Nothing crosses, so nothing evens out.
  3. Burst a cell. In Osmosis, choose an animal cell and Pure water. Water floods in until the cell bursts.
  4. Compare cells. Repeat with a plant cell. It swells a little, then the cell wall holds it firm (turgid). In Very salty water it pulls away from the wall (plasmolysis).

The biology

Diffusion is the net movement of particles from a region of higher concentration to a region of lower concentration, caused by their random motion. It needs no energy. Heating speeds it up.

Osmosis is diffusion of water across a selectively permeable membrane, toward the side with more dissolved solute. A solution with less solute than the cell is hypotonic, one with more is hypertonic, and an equal one is isotonic.
