Try this

  1. Find the best angle. Use Max range, then try 30° and 60°. They land at the same spot. Nothing beats 45°.
  2. Add air. Use the Air drag preset. The path becomes lopsided and the range falls well short of the dashed ideal.
  3. Re-tune it. With drag on, search for the best angle again. It drops below 45°.
  4. Change worlds. Pick the Moon. The flight is six times longer and the range six times greater.

The physics

Without air, the horizontal speed stays constant and the vertical motion is steady acceleration: range = v² sin 2θ / g when launched from the ground. The path is a parabola.

Air drag pushes against the motion with force c·|v|·v, where c = ½ρCdA (kg/m). Wind changes the speed relative to the air. Bounces keep a fraction of the vertical speed, and friction takes away horizontal speed.
