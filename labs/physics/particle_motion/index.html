Try this

  1. Reach equilibrium. Gravity 0, elasticity 1. Watch the bars relax onto the curve as particles collide.
  2. Heat it. Raise Temperature. Average speed and wall pressure climb together.
  3. Squeeze it. Add particles or enlarge them. Collisions per second and pressure rise.
  4. Let it settle. Use the Settling preset: with elasticity below 1, energy is lost and particles pile up.

The physics

Collisions are between equal masses. Along the line joining the centres, the two particles exchange velocity components, so momentum is conserved, and kinetic energy too when elasticity is 1.

Temperature sets the average kinetic energy. Pressure is the momentum the walls receive per second per unit of wall length. The model is two-dimensional and idealised, with no friction or spin.
