The engineering

In steady one-dimensional conduction, heat flux is the same through every layer. Each layer is a thermal resistance R'' = L/k; convection adds R'' = 1/h. The overall temperature drop drives q'' = ΔT / ΣR''.

Building envelopes, furnace walls and electronic packages are often designed with this series-resistance picture before moving to 2D or transient analysis.
