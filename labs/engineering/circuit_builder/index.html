Try this

  1. Series voltages. Load the Series preset. The two resistors share the same current; the larger resistor takes a larger share of the battery voltage.
  2. Parallel currents. Load Parallel. Both resistors see the full battery voltage; currents add at the nodes.
  3. Open the switch. Load With switch, then click the switch to open it. Current falls to zero.
  4. Build your own. Place a battery and two resistors, wire them in series or parallel, and compare the readings.

The engineering

Currents and voltages obey Kirchhoff’s current and voltage laws. The solver builds a conductance matrix (nodal analysis) with the negative terminal of the first battery as ground, then finds every node voltage and branch current.

The model is ideal and direct-current only: no capacitance, inductance, internal resistance or AC behaviour. That keeps the maths transparent while you explore series, parallel and mixed networks.
