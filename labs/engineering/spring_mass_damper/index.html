Try this

  1. Underdamped. Watch the free decay oscillate. Note ωₙ and ζ.
  2. Critical. Raise damping to 2√(km). The mass returns without crossing zero more than once.
  3. Resonance. Apply a force at ω ≈ ωₙ. Amplitude grows until damping balances the drive.
  4. Off resonance. Drive well below ωₙ — steady amplitude is smaller.

The engineering

Natural frequency depends only on mass and stiffness. Damping ratio ζ decides whether the free response oscillates. Forced response peaks near ωₙ when damping is light — the classic resonance problem in machines and structures.

This model is linear and single-DOF. Real systems have many modes, nonlinear stiffness and non-viscous damping; the 1DOF case still teaches the core ideas.
