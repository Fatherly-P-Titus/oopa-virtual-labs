Try this

  1. Forward. Sweep θ₁ and θ₂. The end-effector traces arcs of the workspace.
  2. Inverse. Click a point inside the ring. The arm jumps to a configuration that reaches it.
  3. Elbow up / down. Same target, two solutions — switch the toggle.
  4. Unreachable. Click outside the outer circle or inside the inner hole. IK reports failure.

The engineering

Forward kinematics maps joint angles to Cartesian pose. Inverse kinematics does the reverse and is harder: the same point can often be reached with more than one set of angles (elbow up vs down).

The reachable set for a planar 2R arm is an annulus. This lab is kinematic only — no dynamics, torque limits or collision checking.
