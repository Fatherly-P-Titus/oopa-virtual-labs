  2. Open a path. Delete one diagonal member. The solver should report an unstable mechanism.
  3. Roof loads. Load Roof truss and compare chord forces with the web members.
  4. Beam diagrams. Switch to Beam, load Simple beam, then add a second point load and watch shear and moment change.

The engineering

In a truss, every member carries only axial force. Equilibrium at each joint (ΣFx = 0, ΣFy = 0) determines those forces and the support reactions when the structure is statically determinate (m + r = 2j).

In a beam, bending and shear appear. Vertical equilibrium and moment equilibrium about a support give the reactions; integrating along the span produces the shear force and bending moment diagrams.
