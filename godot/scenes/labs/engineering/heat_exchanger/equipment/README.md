# Equipment presentation

M11.3A and M11.3B provide reusable shell-and-tube and double-pipe 2.5D equipment scenes. Geometry, selection, cutaway, staged separation, and view transitions stay in the presentation layer. M11.4 supplies model outputs to these scenes for representative flow and temperature cues; the equipment scripts do not solve heat transfer.

The double-pipe view distinguishes the inner-tube circuit from the annular circuit. Parallel and counter-current choices change the annular connection ends and the represented flow direction. Shell-and-tube is presented as one shell pass with two tube passes and representative segmental baffles.
