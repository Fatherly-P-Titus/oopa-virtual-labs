YOUR ON-SCREEN GUIDE

Meet Jenny Agu.

Jenny is your guide across OOPA Virtual Labs. She offers short, contextual tips on every page, and you can open or close her at any time with the round button in the lower-right corner.

✦01

Finds your way

Jenny explains each page and points you to the laboratory that fits your interest.

⚠02

Flags what matters

Tips are marked as information, caution or warning, so important notes stand out.

✓03

Respects your time

Tick “Don’t show again” on any tip and Jenny will stop repeating it.
